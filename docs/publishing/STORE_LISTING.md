# Store listing

## English

**Name:** Warframe Tracker

**Short description:**

Plan your next farm with a private inventory tracker, relic assistant, goals,
resources, builds, and live Warframe activity.

**Long description:**

Warframe Tracker turns your collection into a practical farming command center.
Open your in-game inventory and review a private local snapshot before applying
any change. See owned Warframes and weapons, mod quantities, relic refinements,
Prime components, and resources in one place.

Choose a target and discover missing components, related relics, owned
refinements, drop chances, available farming locations, and active Worldstate
opportunities. Track goals, compare equipment, identify nearly completed sets,
and plan lightweight builds without maintaining hundreds of entries by hand.

Your raw inventory snapshot stays in memory and is never uploaded by Warframe
Tracker. The app stores only the normalized collection data you confirm in a
local database on your PC.

**Highlights:**

- automatic inventory preview powered by Overwolf GEP;
- Warframes, weapons, mods, relics, components, and resources;
- Prime farming planner and refined relic quantities;
- goals, buildable sets, comparison, and inventory history;
- Worldstate and farming locations;
- local-first storage and confirmation before changes.
- English-first interface with an optional persistent Spanish language pack.
