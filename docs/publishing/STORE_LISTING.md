# Overwolf Store listing — Native MVP

## English

**Name:** Warframe Tracker

**Short description:**

Turn your confirmed Warframe collection into clear relic, resource, build and
farming plans—in English or Spanish.

**Full description (under 2,000 characters):**

Warframe Tracker turns your collection into a practical farming command center.
It receives inventory snapshots through Overwolf GEP, keeps each snapshot local
until you choose Send, then shows an authenticated preview and requires your
confirmation before storing normalized collection changes.

Search Warframes, weapons, mods, relics, components and resources. See owned and
mastered equipment, independent Intact, Exceptional, Flawless and Radiant relic
quantities, reward probabilities and farming locations. Choose a goal to find
missing components, useful relics you already own and suggested refinements.
Compare equipment, identify nearly completed sets, save lightweight builds and
use current Worldstate activity as farming context.

The interface starts in English and includes a persistent Spanish option.
Warframe Tracker never asks for your Warframe password, reads game memory,
intercepts network traffic, modifies game files or automates gameplay. Inventory
is provided by Overwolf's supported Warframe game-event package. This MVP has no
advertising.

Warframe Tracker is an independent community project and is not affiliated with
or endorsed by Digital Extremes. Warframe and related names and assets are
trademarks of Digital Extremes Ltd.

**Highlights:**

- user-controlled inventory preview powered by Overwolf GEP;
- Warframes, weapons, mods, relics, components and resources;
- Prime farming planner and refined relic quantities;
- goals, builds, comparison and Worldstate context;
- English-first interface with persistent Spanish support.

## Español

Warframe Tracker convierte tu colección confirmada en un centro de mando para
farmear. Relaciona Warframes, armas, mods, reliquias refinadas, componentes y
recursos con tus objetivos. La captura llega mediante Overwolf GEP, permanece
local hasta que decides enviarla y exige una vista previa y confirmación antes
de guardar los cambios normalizados. No solicita credenciales de Warframe, no
lee memoria, no intercepta tráfico y no automatiza el juego.

## Distribution and monetization
