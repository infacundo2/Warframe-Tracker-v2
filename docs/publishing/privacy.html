Warframe Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Summary

Warframe Tracker is a local-first desktop companion. We do not sell personal information, run advertising, or upload your Warframe inventory to our servers.

Inventory data

When automatic inventory capture is enabled, the app receives a snapshot through Overwolf Game Events Provider. The raw JSON is discarded immediately after parsing. A normalized preview remains in memory for no longer than 30 minutes. Item identifiers and quantities that you approve are stored in a SQLite database on your computer.

Account data

The app may store locally the profile name and account totals that are present in an approved source, such as Credits, Endo, Ducats, Aya, and Platinum. Warframe Tracker does not ask for or store your Warframe email, password, two-factor code, or session credentials.

Network requests

The app requests public catalog, Worldstate, and market information from community or game-data APIs. If you voluntarily use the AlecaFrame public link feature, the public token and selected shared data are sent to AlecaFrame as necessary to complete that request. The token is protected at rest by the operating system data-protection mechanism.

Storage and deletion

Desktop data is stored under the Warframe Tracker application-data directory on your PC. You can delete inventory entries from the app. You can remove all local data by uninstalling the app and deleting its application data directory.

Third parties

Overwolf processes game-event data according to its own privacy policy. External public APIs process the requests sent to them. Warframe Tracker is an independent community project and is not affiliated with Digital Extremes.

Children and changes

The app is not directed to children under 13. Material policy changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the Warframe Tracker GitHub repository.
