Warframe Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Summary

Warframe Tracker is an Overwolf Native desktop companion with an authenticated cloud collection. We do not sell personal information or run advertising in the current release.

Inventory data and consent

When automatic inventory capture is enabled, the Native app receives a snapshot through Overwolf Game Events Provider. The raw snapshot remains on the PC for no longer than 30 minutes unless you press Send to Tracker for review. After that explicit action it is sent over HTTPS to the authenticated Tracker service, parsed for a preview, and discarded. Only normalized collection values that you explicitly confirm are stored in the Tracker database.

Account data

The Tracker account stores a profile identifier, authentication data and confirmed collection values, which can include item quantities, mastery and available account totals such as Credits, Endo, Ducats, Aya and Platinum. Warframe Tracker does not ask for or store your Warframe email, password, two-factor code, or Warframe session credentials.

Network requests

The Native shell connects to the Warframe Tracker HTTPS service hosted by our infrastructure. The service may request public catalog, Worldstate and market information from game-data or community APIs. Overwolf processes game events under its own privacy terms. We do not use AlecaFrame credentials or read Warframe process memory.

Storage, retention and deletion

The temporary raw capture is stored locally by the Native shell for up to 30 minutes. Confirmed normalized collection and Tracker account data are stored by the authenticated service. You can discard a local capture before sending it, edit collection entries in the app, and request account and cloud-data deletion through the Support link. Uninstalling removes local app data but does not by itself delete an authenticated cloud account.

Third parties

Overwolf processes game-event and essential technical data according to its privacy policy. External public APIs process only the requests sent to them. Warframe Tracker is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Digital Extremes.

Advertising, children and changes

This release does not display advertising and is not directed to children under 13. If a future version enables advertising, it will use only Overwolf-approved advertising and consent management, and this policy will be updated before activation. Material changes will update the effective date shown above.

Contact

For privacy questions or account/data deletion requests, use the Support page. Never include passwords, tokens, account IDs or raw inventory JSON in a public issue.
